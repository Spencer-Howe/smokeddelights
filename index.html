Smoked Delights is a family owned and operated catering business in Norco, California. BBQ, jerky, cookies and more: everything comes off our smoker and out of our kitchen with care.

Whether it's a birthday for 20 or a corporate event for 800, we build a menu around your crowd, with plant-based options, and we'll even match our table setup to your party's theme.
